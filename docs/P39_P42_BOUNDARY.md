# P39 / P42 boundary

P39 uses “tomography” as a computational strategy for probing pivots, rows,
characters, and exact minors of a fixed relation map. P42 is organized around
a separate tomography paper and may later abstract or reuse that viewpoint.

For the present P39 theorem:

- all definitions, hypotheses, exact artifacts, and proofs are internal to P39;
- no P42 theorem is imported;
- no P42 result is needed to validate the `rho/B1` global gate;
- future synchronization may cite the common methodology without identifying
  the two projects or merging their theorem statements.

This note is the public-package counterpart of the bidirectional bridge notes
maintained in the two internal projects.
