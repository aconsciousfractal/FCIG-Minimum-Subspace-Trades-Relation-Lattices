\section{Full rational generation}
\label{sec:rational-generation}

Let \(C_i\subseteq K_i\) be the rational span of all trades in
\(\cC_i\).  We prove \(C_i=K_i\) by testing the quotient in every
irreducible component.  This section states both the abstract argument
and the exact finite certificate to which it is applied.

\subsection{Component equation matrices}

Let \(k\) be a splitting field for \(\GammaG\) whose characteristic does
not divide \(204\), and let \(V\) be an absolutely irreducible
\(k\GammaG\)-module.  Choose one representative in each block orbit and
write its stabilizer as \(H_j\).  Frobenius reciprocity gives
\[
\Hom_{\GammaG}(k[\cB_i],V)
 \cong \bigoplus_j V^{H_j}.
\]
The analogous point-orbit decomposition gives
\(\Hom_{\GammaG}(k[\cP],V)\).  Put
\[
 m_{B_i}(V)=\dim_k\Hom_{\GammaG}(k[\cB_i],V),
 \qquad
 m_P(V)=\dim_k\Hom_{\GammaG}(k[\cP],V).
\]

Choose minimum-trade orbit seeds \(c_1,\ldots,c_s\).  Evaluation on these
seeds defines the component equation map
\[
 E_{i,V}:\Hom_{\GammaG}(k[\cB_i],V)
 \longrightarrow V^s,
 \qquad
 \phi\longmapsto
 \bigl(\phi(c_1),\ldots,\phi(c_s)\bigr).
\]
Once bases of all fixed spaces have been frozen, this is an ordinary
matrix over \(k\).  Killing one seed kills its entire \(\GammaG\)-orbit,
because \(\phi(gc)=g\phi(c)\).

Every map \(k[\cP]\to V\), pulled back along incidence, kills all
circuits.  Since the incidence map is surjective, this pullback is
injective.  Therefore
\[
 \rank E_{i,V}\le m_{B_i}(V)-m_P(V).
\]

\begin{lemma}[Component rank criterion]
\label{lem:component-rank}
If
\[
 \rank E_{i,V}=m_{B_i}(V)-m_P(V)
\]
for every absolutely irreducible \(V\), then \(C_i=K_i\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The kernel of \(E_{i,V}\) is
\(\Hom_{\GammaG}(k[\cB_i]/C_i,V)\).  Equality in the rank bound says that
this kernel has dimension \(m_P(V)\), exactly the dimension of the
incidence pullback.  Hence
\[
\Hom_{\GammaG}(k[\cB_i]/C_i,V)
 =\Hom_{\GammaG}(k[\cP],V)
\]
for every \(V\).  The induced incidence map
\(k[\cB_i]/C_i\to k[\cP]\) is surjective.  Its source and target have the
same semisimple multiplicities, so it is an isomorphism.  Its kernel is
\((K_i/C_i)\otimes k\), which is therefore zero.  Characteristic-zero
scalar extension gives \(K_i=C_i\).
\end{proof}

\subsection{The exact rank certificate}

The primes \(409\) and \(613\) are congruent to \(1\) modulo \(204\).
Thus both fields split \(\GammaG\).  For each of the three designs and
each of the eight Galois-orbit representatives, the frozen component
matrix has a square minor of order
\[
 m_{B_i}(V)-m_P(V)
\]
whose determinant is nonzero modulo \(409\).  These \(24\) minors prove
the required characteristic-zero ranks.  Reconstructing the same
\(24\) ranks modulo \(613\) is an independent robustness check rather
than an additional premise.

The seed stream is round-robin across all \(69\) two-space orbits.  This
condition is structural: a lexicographic stream can omit invariant mass,
whereas the frozen stream meets every orbit stratum.  The certificate
stores the orbit/stabilizer input, fixed-space bases, selected row and
column indices, determinant residue, and a negative control obtained by
removing one essential row or column.

\begin{theorem}[Minimum trades span the rational kernel]
\label{thm:full-kernel}
For \(i=1,2,3\),
\[
  C_i=K_i.
\]
Moreover, the \(\GammaG\)-spans of deterministic prefixes of respectively
\[
 1\,143,\qquad 1\,170,\qquad 648
\]
minimum-trade seeds already equal \(K_1,K_2,K_3\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The nonzero minors modulo \(409\) attain the upper bound in
\cref{lem:component-rank} for one absolute representative in every
rational Galois orbit.  All circuit vectors are rational, so Galois
conjugation transports each rank statement to the other absolute
constituents in its orbit.  The criterion gives \(C_i=K_i\).

For each design, take the largest prefix length used by any of its eight
component certificates.  Every component has already attained its target
rank at that common prefix, giving the three stated bounds.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The theorem is not a consequence of the census alone.  It depends on
attaining the exact rank in every irreducible component.  Conversely, the
minor at \(409\) is sufficient for the characteristic-zero transfer;
the computation at \(613\) checks the frozen construction independently.
\end{remark}
