\section{Exact certificate contracts}
\label{app:certificates}

The abstract arguments in the paper reduce the locked finite statements to
exact character, rank, determinant, and enumeration inputs.  This appendix
specifies what must be retained for those inputs to be independently
checkable.

\subsection{Characters and relation modules}

The representation certificate contains:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item the \(24\) conjugacy-class representatives and class sizes;
  \item the action convention for \(a,b,c\) and a check of the group laws;
  \item fixed-point counts on \(\cP,\cB_1,\cB_2,\cB_3\);
  \item the absolute character table, Galois grouping, and rational type
  labels;
  \item exact inner products giving the point, block, and kernel
  multiplicities;
  \item dimension and class-function reconstruction checks.
\end{enumerate}
Changing a class label, type ordering, or action convention must invalidate
the reconstruction rather than silently permute a table.

\subsection{Minimum-trade census}

The census contract binds the digests and canonical orderings of the three
block sets.  Every accepted trade records \(Z,U\), the two ordered reguli,
the six lifted blocks, and the canonical unoriented sign convention.
Verification checks:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item all six blocks belong to the selected constituent;
  \item the \(255\) point coordinates balance;
  \item the restricted rank, five-column deletions, and active Smith form;
  \item canonical deduplication and complete \(\GammaG\)-orbit closure;
  \item agreement of template, membership, and orbit-counting passes.
\end{enumerate}
Exhaustiveness is the combination of Krotov's \(t=1\) classification and
complete enumeration of the corresponding \((Z,U)\)-templates.

\subsection{Rational component ranks}

For each pair \((i,V)\), the full-kernel certificate stores:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item block and point orbit representatives with stabilizers;
  \item exact bases of \(V^H\) and the Frobenius-reciprocity coordinate
  convention;
  \item the round-robin seed stream across all \(69\) two-space orbits;
  \item the dimensions \(m_{B_i}(V),m_P(V)\) and target rank;
  \item row and column indices of a target-order square minor;
  \item its nonzero determinant modulo \(409\);
  \item an independent rank/minor reconstruction modulo \(613\);
  \item a deletion or duplication control that loses the certified rank.
\end{enumerate}
There are \(3\cdot8\cdot2=48\) prime/component rank records.  The
\(409\) records are the characteristic-zero witnesses; the \(613\)
records test construction stability.

\subsection{Local saturation}

At \(2\), the witness lists \(66\,674\) explicit circuits per design.
An independent replay reconstructs each point balance and obtains full
rank with the opposite pivot convention.

At \(3\), the contract first verifies the Smith/Bockstein interface:
\[
\rank A_i=254,\qquad
\rank[A_i;\alpha]=255,
\]
together with the unique \(3\)-divisible Smith factor \(120\).  It then
checks the \(F\)-representation laws, Wedderburn mass \(68\), all four
component-family top ranks, and rank-loss mutations.

At \(17\), verification includes free \(C_{17}\)-actions, orbit counts
\(15\) and \(3\,937\), surjectivity on coinvariants, free kernel rank
\(3\,922\), the eight \(C_4\times C_3\) tops, Wedderburn mass \(12\),
and one rank-loss control per component.

For the \(18\) small good-prime maps, the matrices and two selected maximal
minors are reconstructed over the stated integral orders.  Exact
determinants and a Bezout gcd supported on \(\{2,3,17\}\) certify the
localized unit ideal.

\subsection{The \(137\)-component}

The complete-equation contract for \(\rh/\cB_1\) retains:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item the deterministic list of all \(11\,274\) minimum-circuit orbit
  representatives in \(\cB_1\);
  \item the dimension contract \(1317-5=1312\);
  \item a specified \(1312\)-row, \(1312\)-column submatrix of the
  complete equation matrix at \(\zeta_{17}=122\);
  \item an independently reconstructed determinant \(56\bmod137\);
  \item duplicate-column, root-order, and input-hash controls.
\end{enumerate}

For the remaining five large maps, the contract retains:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item all primitive original columns and the entrywise exact-wide replay;
  \item sixteen order-\(17\) roots and, for \(\rh\om\), the sixteen
  Frobenius orbits in \(\FF_{137}[y]/(y^2+y+1)\);
  \item eighty nonzero maximal-order determinant residues in total;
  \item an independent conjugate-root/transposed reconstruction;
  \item eighty duplicate-field-column determinant kills;
  \item the combination with the eighteen small-component certificates.
\end{enumerate}
Every localization contains a unit maximal-order minor, so no local-length
calculation is required.

\subsection{Global \texorpdfstring{\(\rh/\cB_1\)}{rho/B1} structural rank}

The characteristic-zero contract for \cref{thm:global-rho} retains:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item the common \(\ZZ[\zeta_{17}]\) exponent-basis lift of all
  \(1317\) columns and the complete circuit-equation universe;
  \item the exact G-selection binary and manifest, with SHA--256
  \begin{verbatim}

B892C0A7BA9419A8C475FFACF05E63149E5A7F11AEBF443D0F01E96CCB0C96DF
  \end{verbatim}
  and a direct determinant check at \((103,8)\);
  \item the independently selected reverse-row binary and manifest, with
  SHA--256
  \begin{verbatim}
C3F857FDEAE82E8680B3EFB0AA281B59661E04E1E3E7C6D689A28A3FF7F45439
B2D696537A73C30E91D31A8FB3957CEF8F5826ACC8EED66E6881A38AB10EA82B
  \end{verbatim}
  and a direct determinant check at \((137,122)\);
  \item the distinct row and column selection hashes for the two
  order-\(1312\) submatrices of the same complete equation matrix;
  \item every frozen split CRT prime and coefficient residue, the exact
  row-energy bounds, signed uniqueness inequalities, and explicit
  source-admitted status of the CRT generation transcript;
  \item independent cyclotomic multiplication, all sixteen conjugates,
  exact norms, coefficient mutations, and rank-loss controls;
  \item the Smith form of the \(16\times32\) multiplication-lattice
  matrix, with diagonal \(17^{11},289^5\) and index \(17^{21}\);
  \item byte-identical normal and optimized executions of the aggregate
  fail-closed verifier.
\end{enumerate}

The two determinants need not be minors of one fixed \(1312\times1317\)
presentation.  Their common ambient object is the complete equation
matrix, and each therefore belongs to \(I_{1312}(E)\).  Their ideal sum
becomes the unit ideal after \(17\) is inverted.  This closes one
component gate only; it does not certify the other five large maps or a
complete \(\Theta_i\).
