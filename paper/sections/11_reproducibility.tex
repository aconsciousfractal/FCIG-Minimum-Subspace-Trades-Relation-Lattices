\section{Reproducibility and source boundary}
\label{app:reproducibility}

The companion package is organized relative to its repository root.  The
principal fail-closed replay of the characteristic-zero gate is
\begin{verbatim}
python scripts/verify_all.py
python -O scripts/verify_all.py
python scripts/check_manifest.py
\end{verbatim}
It runs four independent verifiers:
\begin{verbatim}
scripts/s449c_verify_g1r_replacement_minor.py
scripts/s449c_verify_g1r_principal_determinant.py
scripts/s449f_verify_reverse_row_determinant.py
scripts/s449f_verify_rho_d1_global_gate.py
\end{verbatim}

The final verifier independently reconstructs the cyclotomic products and
norms, both multiplication lattices, the \(16\times32\) Smith form, and the
G0/reverse-row nonunit negative control.  Its conclusion is restricted to the
complete \(\rh/\cB_1\) component over
\(\ZZ[\zeta_{17},1/(2\cdot3\cdot17)]\).  Generated JSON is canonical, and the
normal and optimized runs are required to be byte-identical.

\paragraph{Evidence tiers.}
The package includes the exact binary minors, CRT inputs and raw outputs needed
for a clean replay of the new global gate.  It also includes frozen compact
receipts and the complete minimum-circuit manifest for the earlier census,
rational-generation, local-saturation, and selected-prime theorem blocks.
The much larger historical raw matrix workspace remains in the source-locked
P39 research archive and is not duplicated in the reviewer-sized package.
Thus the new global theorem has a self-contained deep replay, while the earlier
computer-assisted blocks are distributed with a hash-bound audit trail.  This
distinction is recorded in the companion reviewer guide and claim ledger.

\paragraph{Evidence levels.}
Krotov's classification and the indispensable-fiber criterion are imported
theorems.  The local circuit proof, semisimple criteria, Bockstein
identification, Nakayama reductions, good-prime lemma, CRT uniqueness,
multiplication-lattice interpretation, and Fitting implication are
mathematical arguments in this paper.  The character tables, exhaustive
censuses, component ranks, exact minors, determinant coefficients, Smith
diagonal, and covectors are exact finite inputs with independent replays or
frozen hash-bound receipts.  Resource stops from earlier wide Smith
eliminations are not used as evidence.

\paragraph{Source boundary.}
The prior-art search is dated and bounded.  It found no source giving the same
rational-spanning, cubic-fiber, or complete primary statements at \(137\),
\(219\,097\), and \(3\,288\,036\,131\) for this locked triple, but that negative
result is not a novelty or priority certificate.  The paper therefore makes no
``first'' claim and does not generalize from the three selected configurations
without a separate family argument.  General Fitting, Smith, CRT, and
cyclotomic tools are standard; no method-priority claim is made for their use
in \cref{thm:global-rho}.
