\begin{abstract}
We study the point--block relation modules of three fixed collections of
four-dimensional subspaces of \(\FF_2^8\), each invariant under the same
group of order \(204\).  The minimum point trades are the six-block
opposite-regulus trades.  We determine their exact censuses, prove that
they span the full rational relation module in each of the three
configurations, and identify the complete degree-three layer of the
associated incidence toric ideals.  The minimum trades are locally
saturated at \(2\), \(3\), \(17\), \(137\), \(219\,097\), and
\(3\,288\,036\,131\).  At \(137\), a length-one defect in a row
presentation selected at the control prime \(409\) disappears when all
minimum-circuit orbits are imposed.  The same full-circuit restoration
occurs at \(219\,097\) and \(3\,288\,036\,131\).  Exact maximal-minor
screens, independently replayed at \(128\) localizations over
\(219\,097\) and \(80\) localizations over \(3\,288\,036\,131\), close
the other five large component maps at those primes.
Beyond these finite screens, two exact characteristic-zero maximal minors
prove that the complete \(\rho/\mathcal B_1\) component map is surjective
over \(\mathbb Z[\zeta_{17},1/(2\cdot3\cdot17)]\).  The corresponding
global gates for the other five large maps and the global Smith groups
remain open.
\end{abstract}
